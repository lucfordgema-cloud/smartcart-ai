COLLEGE PROJECT • B.COM

SmartCart AI: Project Overview

An AI-assisted decision layer that helps shoppers move from endless searching to a shortlist that fits their needs and budget.
🎯

Problem Statement

Online shoppers can face too many choices, scattered product details and uncertainty about which option best matches their budget and priorities.

💡

Proposed Solution

SmartCart AI acts as a decision-making layer: it organises product options and gives a reason for a recommendation based on budget, category and preference. The current website demonstrates this with rule-based logic.

🤝

Inclusive Design Goal

The concept aims to make product discovery clearer for budget-conscious shoppers. Future improvements can include multilingual guidance and stronger accessibility support.

⚙️ Key Features

  • Browse products across multiple categories
  • Filter products by search, category and budget
  • Receive rule-based suggestions with a simple fit explanation
  • Compare options by price, category and rating
  • Add items to cart and try a demo checkout
  • View sample order and business metrics in the admin dashboard

💼 Business Model

  • Premium subscription: proposed ₹99/month for advanced decision support, if validated
  • Affiliate commission: potential income from eligible, tracked purchases
  • Brand partnerships: clearly labelled sponsored placements that do not override user fit

📊 Business & Financial Insights

  • Revenue, cost and estimated profit
  • GST shown as a demo checkout calculation
  • Cost of goods sold (COGS) assumptions
  • Affiliate commission and profit margin
  • Order records and basic management reporting
Prototype note: The recommendation feature currently uses predefined rules; it is not connected to a trained AI model. Orders and dashboard figures are stored in this browser for demonstration, not in a secure online database. Financial percentages are illustrative assumptions, not verified business results.
