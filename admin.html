💼 Revenue Model

Multiple potential income streams, subject to product validation.

Premium Subscription

Proposed ₹99/month for advanced comparison and personalisation features. The paid tier should be tested with users before being treated as recurring revenue.

Affiliate Commission

Potential commission only when eligible purchases are made through verified, tracked affiliate links. Clicks and demo cart orders are not proof of earnings.

Brand Partnerships

Potential paid partnerships or sponsored placements. Any sponsored result must be clearly labelled and must not disguise advertising as an independent recommendation.

🧮 Financial Definitions

  • Demo order value: sum of saved order subtotals; not SmartCart's earned revenue.
  • COGS assumption: 60% of demo subtotal for this prototype calculation only.
  • Demo contribution: demo subtotal minus assumed COGS; it excludes actual payroll, marketing, tax and other operating costs.
  • Projected operating surplus: ₹15.68L projected revenue − ₹14.00L projected operating expenses = ₹1.68L.
  • GST: the cart's 18% setting is illustrative, not a determination of the correct tax treatment.
